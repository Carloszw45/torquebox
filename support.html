Customer Support

Contact TorqueBox about an order, delivery, product issue, refund, or privacy question.

Order and delivery assistance

For questions about the status or delivery of an order, email us with the order number and the email address used at checkout. Please describe what you need help with so we can review your request.

Damaged, defective, or incorrect product

If the product arrives damaged or defective, or it is not the product you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery. See our Returns & Refunds Policy. You do not need to return the product for an approved refund.

Delivery estimate

For supported U.S. addresses, our estimated delivery time is 10 to 15 business days. See our Shipping Policy for details.

Privacy requests

To request access, correction, or deletion of personal data where applicable, use the same support email. See our Privacy Policy.

Please do not email complete payment card numbers, passwords, or other sensitive authentication information.
