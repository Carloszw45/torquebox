Standard Operating Procedure

Verified 3-Step Application

No buffing machines or professional training required. Apply in garage or directly at highway rest stops in under two minutes.

01

Mix With Water

Fill a jug with clean water. Add half a bottle (60 mL / 2 fl oz) of QIFOR Wind Shield Pro and mix thoroughly.

02

Fill the Washer Reservoir

Open your vehicle's windshield washer fluid reservoir. Carefully pour in the diluted solution and securely close the cap.

03

Spray & Clean

Activate your windshield washer system to spray the solution onto the glass. Let the wipers sweep away dirt and residue for a clearer view.
