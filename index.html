How to Use

Application in 3 Steps

To use the product in your vehicle’s washer reservoir, prepare the solution in a clean jug and follow the three steps below.

01

Mix with Water

Fill a jug with clean water. Add half a bottle (60 mL / 2.03 fl oz) of QIFOR Wind Shield Pro and mix well.

02

Fill the Washer Reservoir

Open the windshield washer reservoir. Carefully pour in the diluted solution and close the cap.

03

Spray and Wipe

Activate the windshield washer jets to apply the solution to the glass. Let the wiper blades remove dirt and residue.
