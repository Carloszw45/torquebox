30-day issue coverage

Returns & Refunds Policy

This policy applies to TorqueBox orders delivered in the United States.

30-day problem coverage: if a product arrives defective, damaged, or is not the product ordered, contact us within 30 days after delivery. No physical return is required for an approved refund.

What is covered

  • A product with a defect.
  • A product damaged in transit.
  • An incorrect product delivered instead of the item ordered.

This 30-day commercial policy is intended for problems with the product or order. It is not a general change-of-mind or satisfaction guarantee. Nothing here limits rights provided by applicable consumer protection law.

How to request a refund

  1. Email suporte@torque-box.com within 30 days after receiving the product.
  2. Include your order number and a description of the problem. Photos, if available, can help us review your request.
  3. We will review the issue and reply with the outcome and next steps.

Refunds and returns

If your request qualifies, TorqueBox will initiate a refund for the affected product to the original payment method within 5 business days after approval. You will not need to ship the product back. Business days exclude weekends and U.S. public holidays. The time until the funds appear in your account may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.

Orders not delivered or canceled

If your order has not arrived or shipment is delayed, contact us. Cancellation and refund rights for unshipped orders remain available as required by applicable law. See our Shipping Policy for delivery estimates.

Questions? Email suporte@torque-box.com.
