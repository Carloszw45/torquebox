Where we deliver

TorqueBox products are stocked in the United States. This estimate applies to deliveries within the United States. Availability at checkout determines whether a particular delivery address is supported.

Delivery estimates

The estimated delivery period is 10 to 15 business days after the order is confirmed. Business days exclude Saturdays, Sundays, and U.S. public holidays. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed arrival date.

Shipping charges

Any applicable shipping charges will be shown during checkout before you confirm your order.

Delays or missing orders

If a shipment is delayed or cannot be sent within the applicable timeframe, we will contact you about the revised timing and your available options, including cancellation and a refund where required. If you have not received your order within the estimated period, please contact us with your order number.

Damaged or incorrect products

For products that arrive damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered, please review our Returns & Refunds Policy.
