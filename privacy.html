Privacy Policy

This policy explains how TorqueBox handles personal information when you browse the store, place an order, or contact us.

Who is responsible

TorqueBox operates this online store from Brazil and serves customers in the United States. For questions about how personal information is handled, email suporte@torque-box.com.

Information we collect

  • Order information: your name, email address, shipping address, items ordered, order value, and optional phone number when provided.
  • Payment and order status: payment confirmation, transaction identifiers, refunds, and fraud-prevention information made available by our payment processors. We do not request complete card details by email.
  • Support communications: information you provide when requesting help or making a refund or privacy request.
  • Shopping cart: selected products and quantities saved in your browser's local storage so your cart can be restored when you revisit.
  • Technical data: hosting and service providers may process IP address, device/browser information, and access or security logs needed to deliver and protect the website.

How we use this information

We use personal information to handle orders, verify payments, deliver products, provide support, process eligible refunds, prevent fraud, maintain the website, and comply with legal obligations.

If we introduce abandoned-cart reminders or promotional emails, we will offer a separate choice to subscribe where required. You may withdraw marketing consent or unsubscribe from those messages. Order and service messages may still be sent as necessary for purchases you make.

Local storage and third-party services

Our website uses browser local storage for the shopping cart. The website may load fonts and site scripts from third-party providers. Payment processors, website hosting providers, and delivery partners may process information needed to provide their respective services. Available payment methods and processors are shown during checkout; card payments may be handled by Stripe when that integration is enabled.

Sharing and international processing

We share information only as needed with service providers involved in hosting, payment processing, order fulfillment, delivery, security, customer support, and compliance with the law. Because our business operates in Brazil and serves U.S. customers, data may be processed across borders where permitted by applicable law.

Retention and protection

We keep personal data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, handle disputes, meet recordkeeping obligations, and protect the business and customers. We use reasonable safeguards and limit access where appropriate.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or object to certain uses. Please contact suporte@torque-box.com and describe your request. Some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve transactions.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as the website and payment options evolve. Any new version will be posted on this page with its update date.
