Car Drying Air Blower

Find the water hiding in the details.

  • Keep the nozzle clear while directing the airflow.

Clear trapped water from the gaps a towel cannot reach.

Your panels look dry, then a mirror or badge releases another line of water.

Fewer overlooked wet spots when you finish the drying stage.

WHEN TO USE ITAfter rinsing, before finishing

KNOW THE ROUTINE

Good care.
Clearly shown.

See the preparation, application and finishing steps for this reference product.

  1. Set up the dryer

    Follow the manual, fit the suitable nozzle and keep the unit and electrical connections in a dry working area.

  2. Work through the gaps

    Guide airflow through mirrors, badges and grilles from the top down. Keep the nozzle off the surface.

  3. Finish the panels

    Use a clean microfiber drying towel where water remains, then check for late drips.

THE DETAIL THAT MATTERS

Use a clean towel for water left on larger panels. Confirm voltage and plug before choosing a final model.
