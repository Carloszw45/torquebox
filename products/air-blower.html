YOUR ROUTINE

How to use it

  1. Prepare a suitable area

    Follow the dryer instructions. Keep electrical connections and the unit away from standing water.

  2. Work from the top down

    Direct the airflow into mirrors, badges and trim while keeping the nozzle clear of the surface.

  3. Finish the panels

    Collect remaining water with a clean microfiber towel and check for drips.
