YOUR ROUTINE

How to use it

  1. Mix as directed

    Use a suitable foam product and follow its dilution instructions.

  2. Apply to a cool exterior

    Work out of direct sun. Cover the surface and allow the label-specified dwell time.

  3. Rinse before hand washing

    Do not let the foam dry. Rinse off, then continue with your contact wash.

Good to know

Is it a complete car wash by itself?

Foam is a pre-wash step. A contact wash may still be needed for remaining dirt.

No. This is an accessory for a compatible washer. Final fittings and included items will be confirmed at launch.
