KNOW THE ROUTINE

Good care.
Clearly shown.

See the preparation, application and finishing steps for this reference product.

  1. Prepare the mixture

    Add water and a compatible foaming product using its bottle directions. Do not choose a ratio by eye.

  2. Cover the cool panels

    Connect to a compatible pressure washer and spread the foam evenly, away from direct sun.

  3. Rinse before it dries

    Respect the product dwell time. Rinse thoroughly, then wash by hand when needed.
