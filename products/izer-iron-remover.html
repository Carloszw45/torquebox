Target iron contamination a regular wash can leave behind.

Some particles bonded to wheels and paint are not ordinary loose road dirt.

KNOW THE ROUTINE

Good care.
Clearly shown.

See the preparation, application and finishing steps for this reference product.

  1. Confirm and cool the surface

    Check material compatibility. Do not use on zinc-coated or hot surfaces.

  2. Apply and allow the reaction

    The manufacturer specifies 2–3 minutes. Keep the product from drying.

  3. Agitate and rinse fully

    Use a suitable tool gently as directed, then rinse with an appropriate pressure washer.

THE DETAIL THAT MATTERS

Manufacturer dwell guidance is 2–3 minutes. A purple/darker reaction can indicate iron contamination; do not let the product dry.

No. It targets iron contamination and does not replace the normal wash stage.
