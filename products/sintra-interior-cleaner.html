See the preparation, application and finishing steps for this reference product.

  1. Check and test

    Confirm compatibility and test an inconspicuous area. Use the Fast version as supplied.

  2. Apply and loosen

    Use the label method and a suitable soft brush or applicator to lift the dirt.

    For trim, the manufacturer directs a damp microfiber followed by a dry one. Let fabrics dry completely.

Sintra Fast is ready to use. Do not confuse it with Sintra Pro concentrate. Cleaning directions do not imply instant disinfection.
