KNOW THE ROUTINE

Good care.
Clearly shown.

See the preparation, application and finishing steps for this reference product.

  1. Apply to a dry tire

    Start with a cool, dry sidewall. Apply the foaming cleaner as directed.

  2. Dwell, then scrub

    The manufacturer specifies 30–60 seconds before brushing thoroughly with a suitable tire brush.

  3. Rinse and let it dry

    Remove all product with water. Dry the sidewall before applying dressing.

THE DETAIL THAT MATTERS

Manufacturer dwell guidance is 30–60 seconds. Foam can darken as dirt is lifted; it is not a tire dressing.
