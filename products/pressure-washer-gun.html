YOUR ROUTINE

How to use it

  1. Check the connections

    Compare your washer hose, inlet and outlet fittings. Add a compatible adapter if needed.

  2. Connect with the washer off

    Fit the hose and an appropriate nozzle, then check that the connections are secure.

  3. Rinse with care

    Follow the washer instructions and keep a suitable distance from paint, trim and seals.

BEFORE YOU START

Good to know

Does it work with a garden hose?

This is a pressure-washer accessory. A garden hose alone cannot power it.

Will it fit every pressure washer?

No. Check fittings and pressure ratings; some machines need an adapter. The final offered model will be confirmed before launch.
