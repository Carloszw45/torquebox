See the preparation, application and finishing steps for this reference product.

  1. Match the fittings

    Check your washer hose connection, working pressure and required adapters before assembly.

  2. Connect with power off

    Attach the hose and a suitable nozzle securely. Follow the pressure-washer instructions.

  3. Rinse with control

    Use an appropriate spray pattern and distance for paint, trim and seals. Never aim at people.
