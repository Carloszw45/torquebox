THE DETAILS MAKE THE DIFFERENCE

Clear everyday haze from automotive glass.

Fingerprints and a thin interior haze can make freshly washed glass still look unfinished.

A straightforward way to finish the glass as carefully as the rest of the car.

WHEN TO USE ITInterior or exterior glass cleaning
MADE FORCompatible automotive glass
KNOW THE ROUTINE

Good care.
Clearly shown.

See the preparation, application and finishing steps for this reference product.

  1. Apply a small amount

    Work on cool, compatible glass and use only the amount directed on the bottle.

  2. Spread with a glass cloth

    Use a dedicated microfiber to work the cleaner across the glass promptly.

  3. Turn to a clean side

    Finish with the dry side or a second cloth. Repeat locally if residue remains.

THE DETAIL THAT MATTERS

Use a dedicated glass cloth. The manufacturer advises a second cloth or a clean side for the finishing pass.

BEFORE YOU START

A little more clarity.

Does it replace windshield washer fluid?

No. It is applied directly when cleaning glass, not added to the washer reservoir.

Can I use it on aftermarket tint?

Check the tint manufacturer guidance and the product label before using any glass cleaner.
