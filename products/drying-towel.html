YOUR ROUTINE

How to use it

  1. Start with clean paint

    Wash and rinse thoroughly. Do not use a drying towel to wipe away dirt.

  2. Let the fibers do the work

    Lay the towel flat and pat or pull it gently over the panel without scrubbing.

  3. Wash and air-dry

    Use a suitable microfiber detergent. Avoid fabric softener and high heat.
