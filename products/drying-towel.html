A CLEAR ROUTINE

Know what to do.
Then enjoy the finish.

Three practical steps for the reference product.
Illustrated application guide.

  1. 1

    Rinse the dirt away

    Start with a washed and thoroughly rinsed car. A drying towel should pick up water, not road grit.

  2. 2

    Lay it flat and lift

    Place the towel on a panel, then pat or draw it gently towards you. Wring it out when needed.

  3. 3

    Care for the fibers

    Wash with a suitable microfiber detergent and air-dry. Avoid fabric softener and high heat.

Use a dedicated clean towel. Skip fabric softener when washing microfiber.
