Three practical steps for the reference product.
Illustrated application guide.

  1. 1

    Choose and measure

    Match the label dilution to the dirt: reference guidance ranges from 1:100 to 1:10.

  2. 2

    Wash from the top

    Work on cool surfaces with a suitable microfiber mitt and light passes.

  3. 3

    Rinse and dry fully

    Remove all product, then dry the vehicle to limit water left on the exterior.

Manufacturer reference ratios: 1:100 for light soil, 1:50 for medium soil and 1:10 for heavy soil. Follow your bottle.
