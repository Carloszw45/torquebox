YOUR ROUTINE

How to use it

  1. Check the surface

    Confirm compatibility with your wheel finish. Work on cool surfaces.

  2. Apply and agitate

    Use the label method and an appropriate brush without letting the cleaner dry.

  3. Rinse thoroughly

    Remove all residue. Take extra care around electrical components in engine bays.

BEFORE YOU START

Good to know

Will it remove every bonded contaminant?

Not necessarily. Bonded iron or tar may require a suitable dedicated product.

Can I use it on hot wheels?

Allow the surface to cool and follow the product directions first.
