Three practical steps for the reference product.
Illustrated application guide.

  1. 1

    Select the surface and ratio

    Check compatibility, test an inconspicuous area and measure the label-approved dilution.

  2. 2

    Apply and loosen the soil

    Use a suitable sprayer and soft brush or applicator. For fabrics, avoid unnecessary saturation.

  3. 3

    Remove and let it dry

    Rinse appropriate exterior areas, or wet-extract approved fabrics. Let fabrics dry completely.

Manufacturer ratios vary by job, including 1:10 heavy, 1:30 medium and 1:50 maintenance. Use the exact label and equipment guidance.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions, answered.

Can I use it on leather seats or plastic trim?

The manufacturer does not recommend Impact for leather seats or interior/exterior plastics. Choose a suitable dedicated cleaner instead.

Can I use the same dilution everywhere?

No. The concentration and removal method depend on the surface, dirt level and equipment. Follow the exact bottle directions.
